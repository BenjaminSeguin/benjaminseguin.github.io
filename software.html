Software

Open-source packages

Three R packages for econometric inference, written with David Ardia at HEC Montréal and published on CRAN. Each has an accompanying paper: see Publications.

  • R · CRAN

    RSDC

    Regime-switching dynamic correlation models

    A framework for modelling, estimating and forecasting correlation structures in multivariate time series under regime-switching dynamics. Extends the Pelletier (2006) framework to allow time-varying transition probabilities driven by exogenous variables, alongside the fixed-probability case.

    spantest

    Mean-variance spanning tests

    A comprehensive suite of portfolio spanning tests: the classical procedures of Huberman and Kandel (1987), Gibbons, Ross and Shanken (1989), Kempf and Memmel (2006), Gungor and Luger (2016) and Pesaran and Yamagata (2024), together with the residual-based subseries tests of Ardia and Sessinou (2024). Supports asset-level and global hypotheses, with a Cauchy combination test for robust inference under serial dependence, heteroskedasticity and high dimension.

  • R · CRAN

    PeerPerformance

    Luck-corrected peer performance analysis

    Pairwise comparison of fund performance — Sharpe and modified Sharpe ratios, screening across large peer groups — with a false-discovery-rate correction that separates genuine skill from luck.
