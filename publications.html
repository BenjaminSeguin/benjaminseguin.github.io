    Nonlinear Conjugate Gradient with Perturbed Heavy-Ball Saddle Escape: Complexity and Local Convergence

    Benjamin Séguin, Clément W. Royer

    Manuscript in preparation

    A first-order method for smooth nonconvex optimization combining a safeguarded nonlinear conjugate gradient descent phase with a perturbed heavy-ball escape phase. Returns an $(\varepsilon_g,\varepsilon_H)$-second-order stationary point with high probability under gradient- and Hessian-Lipschitz continuity alone, using gradient evaluations only and no negative-curvature oracle. Includes a local convergence rate under the strict saddle property.
